Residential tenancy agreement

2. Term. The tenancy begins on 1 January 2027 and continues for twelve months, after which it becomes a rolling monthly tenancy unless either party gives two months' written notice.

3. Rent. Rent is payable monthly in advance on the first day of each month. The first payment covers January 2027. Rent may be reviewed once in any twelve-month period, with at least two months' notice of any change.

4. Deposit. A deposit equal to five weeks' rent is held in a government-approved scheme. The landlord will provide the scheme's reference within thirty days of receiving the deposit.

5. Use of the property. The property is to be used as a private residence only. The tenant may keep one cat with the landlord's written consent, which will not be unreasonably withheld.

6. Repairs. The landlord is responsible for the structure and exterior, heating, hot water, and installations for the supply of water, gas and electricity. The tenant must report faults promptly and allow access for repairs with 24 hours' notice.
